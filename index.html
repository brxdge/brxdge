WHO ARE WE?

Built by creators, run for creators.

BRXDGE started as a group chat between three friends helping their favorite local creators land their first brand deals. Today we manage a growing roster of talent across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube — handling everything from content strategy to contract negotiation, so our talent can focus on making things people actually want to watch. We exist to bridge the gap between raw talent and the opportunities it deserves.

We're hands-on, relationship-first, and allergic to cookie-cutter management. Every creator on our roster gets a strategy built around their audience, not a template.
